In Memoriam

They finished their watch in the homes of the people they worked for. We keep their names here.

🐾

2011 – 2024
Chula Vista Police Dept.
Nine years on patrol. Six years on a couch.
🐾
Tank
2010 – 2023
National City Police Dept.
Narcotics detection. Retired to his handler's family.
🐾
Boomer
2009 – 2022
San Diego County Sheriff
Two Medal of Valor deployments.
🐾
Ranger
2012 – 2025
Coronado Police Dept.
Explosives detection. Slept on the porch to the end.
🐾
Zeus
2008 – 2021
Chula Vista Police Dept.
The first K-9 SCCA ever helped.
🐾
Molly
2013 – 2025
Imperial Beach
Tracking. Retired with her handler in 2023.
Taking care of those who took care of us.
