Our Mission

These K‑9s protected our communities. Now we protect them.

A police K‑9 retires into the home of the handler who worked beside it. From that day on, every X‑ray, surgery, and prescription is the sole responsibility of that family.

Peer‑reviewed research shows retired police K‑9s face significantly higher rates of orthopedic injuries, musculoskeletal disease, and dental issues due to the physical demands of their service. Belgian Malinois and German Shepherds are also at elevated risk for life‑threatening emergencies like GDV (bloat) — surgeries that can cost $10,000–$20,000.

SCCA pays the vet directly. No GoFundMe. No waiting.
