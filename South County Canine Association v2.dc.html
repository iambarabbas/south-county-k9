Why we exist

When the vest comes off, the bills come home.

A police K-9 retires into the house of the handler who worked beside it. From that day on, every X-ray, surgery and prescription is paid out of one family’s pocket.

These dogs have working bodies — hips, spines and teeth spent on years of tracking and apprehension. We pay the vet directly, so nobody has to run a fundraiser for their own dog.
