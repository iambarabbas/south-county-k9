The Roster

Our K‑9s

We are wholeheartedly dedicated to caring for retired police canines who bravely served our communities. Their handlers keep them — we keep them healthy.

Active care
Rocko
Belgian Malinois
National City Police Dept. · Retired 2023
K‑9 Rocko is a 9‑year‑old Belgian Malinois who worked for the National City Police Department and served the community for 6 years. Rocko was trained as a patrol canine and article detection specialist. He retired in 2023 after several loyal years of service and now enjoys retirement with his handler.
Active care
Cooper
Labrador Retriever
South County Narcotics Unit · Retired
K‑9 Cooper was a narcotics detection K‑9 with a South County narcotics unit. Cooper played a valuable role in finding and removing hundreds of pounds of narcotics off the streets as well as intercepting shipments through the mail. Cooper is fun‑loving and looking forward to many years of snuggles with his handler.
Active care
Chon
Belgian Malinois
South County Law Enforcement · Retired 2021
K‑9 Chon is an 8‑year‑old Belgian Malinois who served nearly four years in patrol as an apprehension K‑9. Throughout his career, Chon demonstrated unwavering loyalty, bravery, and determination. In 2021, he was retired early after sustaining a heat stroke. Since retirement, Chon has transitioned from the kennel to a house dog and quickly adapted to the comforts of being a family companion.
Active care
Turbo
K‑9
South County Law Enforcement · 13 years old
K‑9 Turbo is 13 years old and was in service for 7 years. In retirement, Turbo enjoys hiking in the mountains and has a talent for finding shed deer antlers along the trail.
Active care
Jax
Belgian Malinois
South County Law Enforcement · Retired
K‑9 Jax is a 9‑year‑old Belgian Malinois who served four years in patrol as an apprehension K‑9. Jax demonstrated great bravery during his service, apprehending very dangerous criminals in the South County community. He played an intricate part in keeping his handler, fellow officers, and the community safe. In retirement, Jax loves chewing on his Kong toys, enjoys belly rubs from his family, and chasing lizards in the backyard.
Active care
Mattis
Belgian Malinois (Mexi-malinois)
South County Law Enforcement · 7.5 years old
Retired apprehension and detection K‑9 Mattis is a 7.5‑year‑old Mexi‑malinois with an impressive record: 30 criminal apprehensions, hundreds of surrenders, and multiple firearms, ammunition, and cash seizures. In retirement, Mattis enjoys floating in the pool, driving down the coast with the windows down, and munching on carne asada tacos.
“Taking care of those who took care of us.”
